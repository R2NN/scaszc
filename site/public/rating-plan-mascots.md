# Иллюстрация состава и плана

Используется `rating-plan-mascots-v2.webp` в `src/StaffingPlanPanel.jsx`.
Создана встроенным image_gen с прозрачным фоном. Референсы персонажей: `public/avatars/bee-static.png` и `public/avatars/cat-static.png`.
Первый вариант с рыжим котом отклонён пользователем; в интерфейсе используется только v2.

Промпт:

> Create a NEW small product illustration using EXACTLY the two mascot characters in the reference images. Image 1 is the character reference for BeeGo's round yellow bee, black hair cap and black antennae, white wings and white gloves, dispatcher headset with microphone, simple solid oval black eyes. Image 2 is the character reference for BeeGo's white cat with black patch on upper right of its head, yellow inside ears, yellow neckerchief tied on its left side (image right), black oval eyes, tiny yellow mouth. Preserve their distinctive proportions, faces, colors, thick smooth black contours and simple flat vector-like cartoon style. Place both friendly mascots together, looking at and holding one unfolded paper map with a simple purple route connecting three yellow pins, a quiet product onboarding scene. Cat on left, bee on right. Small compact illustration, no background, genuine alpha transparency, centered full composition with small margins. Absolutely no orange fur, no tabby markings, no safety vests, no realistic fur, no 3D, no painterly shading, no big sparkly eyes. No text or letters. Match the supplied mascot drawings closely, do not redesign either character.

Изображение уменьшено до 640×640 и сохранено WebP с альфа-каналом. Исходные аватары не менялись. WebP-версии аватаров и портретов сохраняют прежнее сопоставление идентификаторов и кадрирование; JPG/PNG оставлены как исходники.
